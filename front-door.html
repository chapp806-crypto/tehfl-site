Not what sounds important. Not what you think you should talk about. Just the thing that naturally keeps asking for your attention.

3 • The Signal Under the Signal

What may be underneath the first thing you noticed?

A feeling. A fear. A need. A pattern. An unfinished conversation. Stay curious without forcing certainty.

4 • Identify Needs

What might this be asking from you right now?

Attention? Information? Rest? Support? A boundary? A conversation? Action? More time?

5 • Nurture the Long Harvest

What needs time? Allow it.

Some things need movement. Some need room. What could you stop forcing long enough to observe?

6 • Bring it with you

What would you want to talk through with real people?

You do not need a conclusion. Bring the thing that still has energy, tension, curiosity, or uncertainty around it.
